Meet your coach

The coach Vadodara's swimmers call Sanjay sir

I'm Sanjay Kumar, Head Coach at Baroda Swim Front in Gotri. I've coached for over five years, from kids taking their first dip to school swimmers chasing medals at inter-school and district meets.

The way I teach is simple. Safety first. Confidence next. Technique that holds up when you're tired. Every swimmer gets a plan for their own body and their own goal, and we track it every week.

I hold a National Certificate in Swimming and I'm certified in water safety and lifesaving. I teach in English and Hindi, so nothing gets lost.

Questions

Asked at the poolside

Still unsure? Message me. I reply to every one.

Do you give personal swimming training at home in Vadodara?

Yes. If you have access to a pool at home, in your society or apartment, at a farmhouse or a club, I come to you. Timings are planned around your day.

Where are the swimming classes held?

Batches and pool sessions run at Baroda Swim Front, at Aark International School in Gotri, Vadodara. Personal training can also happen at your own pool.

What age can my child start swimming lessons?

Most kids are ready from around 5 years old, once they can follow simple instructions in the water. Message me and we will talk about your child.

I'm an adult and scared of deep water. Can I still learn?

Yes. Many of my students started as adults who were afraid of the water. We begin in the shallow end, work on breathing and floating, and only move deeper when you are ready.

How long does it take to learn swimming?

It depends on your comfort in water and how often you come. With regular sessions, most beginners float and swim short distances on their own within a few weeks.

What are the fees for swimming classes?

Fees depend on the program, the number of sessions, and whether I train you at your own pool. Send me a message on WhatsApp and I will share the current fees.

Do you coach swimmers for competitions?

Yes. I coach school and club swimmers on starts, dives, turns, breathing and race pace for inter-school, district and state level meets.

Which languages do you teach in?

English and Hindi.
